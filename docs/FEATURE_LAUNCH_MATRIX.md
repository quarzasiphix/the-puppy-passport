## What this means for beta scope

Every customer-facing product workflow (marketplace, applications, transport, messaging, support,
moderation, ownership) is real, tested, and RLS-hardened — not mocked, not a UI shell in front of
nothing. The only things genuinely missing are **payments, analytics/CRM, email, and SMS
provider integrations** — and those aren't half-built with a bypassable flag; they simply don't
exist in the codebase yet, so there's no "disabled feature" attack surface to close for them.
Fundraising is the one deliberately-flagged-off feature, and its client-side-only gate was already
proven safe (no real money can move regardless) rather than left as an assumption.
